About WolfPack

+

Do I need to be a US citizen to use Wolfpack?

No. Wolfpack is available for all users globally. However, only US citizens and US residents can set up a brokerage account and trade stocks and ETFs within the app.

+

What is the difference between a Wolfpack account and a brokerage account?

A Wolfpack account allows you to use the Wolfpack app and discovery Stocks and ETFs that each your investment criteria. A brokerage account allows you to trade stocks and ETFs within the app.

+

What is our discovery feature?

Allows you to define your own personalized investment profile(s), by selecting several investment filters. Activating each filter based on its level of importance to you (Mandatory, High, Medium, Low). Once a discovery profile is defined, Wolfpack will use its investment criteria to match with Stocks and ETFs, provided with a percentage match based on your discovery profile(s).

+

What is our auto top-up feature?

Auto top-up automatically deposit funds into your TradingBlock account in order to grow your investment portfolio. You can activate multiple concurrent top-ups in two ways: 1. Setup an auto top-up and define an amount you wish to deposit including the frequency and duration. 2. Setup an auto top-up when you buy stocks or ETFs, so that you can replenish your brokerage account with what you used to buy your investment.

No. Wolfpack is available for all users globally. However, Yes. Anyone can download Wolfpack from the App Store or Google Play and sign up to use the app. However, only US citizens and US residents can open a brokerage account.

Are my funds and investments safe with Wolfpack?

Wolfpack uses TradingBlock as our broker dealer. TradingBlock is a member of the Securities Investor Protection Corporation (SIPC). Under SIPC your account is protected in the event of a brokerage firm failure or liquidation. The SIPC limit covers $500,000 of securities of which $250,000 can be in cash. All investments carry risk of loss. SIPC does not provide protection against a loss in market value of your investments..

I sold a security, now when can I transfer my funds back to my account?

Fund transfer requests are processed the same day if received before 4PM ET on regular business days.

What is a Cash Account?

Your brokerage account is a cash account which means that your investments must be paid for in full using settled funds by the settlement date of the trade. TradingBlock requires that you have cash available in your account prior to placing trades. Additionally, when sale proceeds are used for new purchases prior to the settlement date of the sale, you are agreeing in good faith to hold the new investment at least until the settlement date of the sale.

How long will it take for my trades to settle?

The purchase and sale of securities is subject to a trade settlement period that is typically 2 business days from the trade date known as (T+2).

+

What are “Unsettled Funds”?

The cash proceeds from the sale of a stock or ETF investment are considered “unsettled funds” for 2 business days following the sale. The cash from an investment sale appears in your account immediately but is unsettled for 2 business days.

+

Am I allowed to reinvest unsettled funds?

Yes! You are allowed to reinvest unsettled funds for new investments. In so doing you are agreeing in good faith to hold your new investment until at least the settlement date of the cash used.

+

What is a Good Faith Violation?

A good faith violation (GFV) occurs when you invest using unsettled funds and sell the new investment prior to the settlement date of the cash used. If you incur 3 GFVs within a 12-month period, your account will be restricted from using unsettled funds for new investments. You will be required to wait for funds to settle before reinvesting the cash.

What price will I pay when I buy a dollar amount of a security?

Orders are processed during regular US market hours as market orders. A market order instructs the broker to buy or sell securities for your account at the next available market price. It remains in effect on the business day entered, and usually results in the prompt purchase or sale of all the shares of stock in question, if the security is actively traded, and market conditions permit.

Orders placed when the regular US market session is closed will “Pend” until they are fully processed on the system before the start of trading on the next business day. Please check the status of your orders prior to the market open on the next business day.

Dividends & Taxes

+

What is a dividend?

A distribution of a portion of a company profit to its shareholders. The payment of the dividend is decided by the board of directors.

+

The security I own pays a dividend, when will I receive it?

To record the entitlement to a dividend, companies establish key dates associated with the dividend payment to shareholders. The “record date” establishes the holders of a security entitled to receive the dividend payment. The “ex-date” is the date that the security price is reduced by the amount of the dividend to reflect the amount of the dividend being paid. The “pay date” is the date that investors receive the dividend payment into their account. If you own a security prior to the ex-date of a dividend, then you will typically receive the dividend on the pay date.

+

Do you withhold taxes from my account?

There are typically no tax withholdings from investment sales in an account if the correct W8 tax information is on file. Dividends are typically taxed when received into your account. For more information, please consult with a tax advisor.

+

Will I receive a tax form for my investment activity?

Yes, if you generate tax qualifying events during the calendar year, then you will receive a tax form from TradingBlock in February of the following year. Tax forms are available on the mobile app in the same location as your account statements and trade confirmations.
